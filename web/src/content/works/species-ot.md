Exploring how optimal transport can map mouse cells to human cells in a shared autoencoder latent space.

A command-line toolkit brings model comparison, scorecards, and cluster workflows together. It lists models, prints a scorecard, and shows the sbatch chain a human still submits.

This work is in progress. Research results stay outside the public repository.
